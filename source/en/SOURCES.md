# Sources and provenance

[← English documentation](README.md) · [Türkmençe](../tm/SOURCES.md)

Dataset snapshot: **2026-08-21**. Primary sources take precedence for legal-status and name changes. Secondary sources structure published census material and cross-check relationships and transliterations.

## Primary sources

1. [Mejlis resolution on administrative-territorial division](https://www.turkmenistan.gov.tm/en/post/98762/resolution-mejlis-turkmenistan-administrative-territorial-division-turkmenistan), 2025-09-19 — establishment and composition of seven etraps.
2. [State Committee on Statistics — 2022 census](https://www.stat.gov.tm/en/population-census), published 2024 — primary reference for the 2022 administrative structure.
3. [Law on administrative-territorial division matters](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/255?lang=tm), consolidated through 2023-06-03 — legal definitions of administrative categories.
4. [2026 election notice](https://www.saylav.gov.tm/election?election=1) — confirms administrative use of the newly established etraps.
5. [Decree concerning Arkadag](https://www.turkmenistan.gov.tm/en/post/71147/president-turkmenistan-signed-decree-renaming-hakimlik-city-arkadag), 2023-03-30 — confirms its city-of-state-significance status.

## Secondary cross-checks

6. [Districts of Turkmenistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Districts_of_Turkmenistan), accessed 2026-08-21 — etrap relationships.
7. [Cities of Turkmenistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cities_of_Turkmenistan), accessed 2026-08-21 — census-based city list.
8. [Towns of Turkmenistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towns_of_Turkmenistan), accessed 2026-08-21 — list of 71 towns.
9. [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), Overpass snapshot 2026-08-21 — names, coordinates, district tags, and available population fields. OSM data is under ODbL and does not replace an official register.
10. [Districts in Turkmenistan](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Districts_in_Turkmenistan), accessed 2026-08-21 — 467 rural councils and 1,615 council-to-village relationships derived from a legal compilation updated through 2025.

## Limitations

- Every region record stores `source_url` and `source_date`.
- Coordinates are not invented; missing values remain `NULL`.
- Systematically transliterated Russian names may require verification. Former Russian/Soviet names are stored as historical aliases.
- Parent relationships not explicit in a primary source are marked `verification_required`.
- Rural-council and village layers combine an administrative compilation with OSM. Their count may exceed the official total and they must not be presented as an official register.
