# Sources and Rights

## Candidate source: Kitaphana

- Website: [kitaphana.net](https://www.kitaphana.net/)
- Role: discovery catalog and possible source location
- Reviewed: 2026-08-23
- Full-text import status: **not authorized**

The website exposes a large catalog, including Turkmen fiction, folk tales, children's books, and works by modern authors. Its public pages display a site copyright notice, but no general open-data or open-content license permitting bulk redistribution or LLM training was found during review.

No full text from Kitaphana may be committed to this repository until the applicable work and edition are confirmed as public domain, explicitly openly licensed, or covered by written permission. A `robots.txt` rule, public download button, or ability to read a document online is not a copyright license.

## Required source record

For every imported work, document:

- source title and URL;
- author, translator, editor, and publisher when known;
- original and edition publication dates;
- retrieval date;
- copyright status and jurisdictional basis;
- license name and URL, or a reference to written permission;
- OCR/extraction method and verification status.

Source-specific entries should be added below this section before their text is added to `data/`.
