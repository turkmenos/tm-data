# Data Sources

The dataset is compiled from publicly available official and open-data sources.

Detailed source information and methodology for the geography dataset are available in:

- [English sources](geo/source/en/SOURCES.md)
- [Türkmençe çeşmeler](geo/source/tm/SOURCES.md)

Official government and statistical sources are preferred where available. Additional geographic data may be sourced from OpenStreetMap and is provided with the appropriate attribution.

Each record includes source and verification information where available. Other datasets must document their own provenance in their folder; missing source documentation is a known limitation and must not be interpreted as permission to redistribute third-party material.

**Dataset version:** 2026-08-21
